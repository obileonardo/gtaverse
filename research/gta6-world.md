## 3. Regions and counties

Leonida is Rockstar's fictional Florida. The game uses two layers: six headline **regions** (marketing and community names) and five **counties** (shown on the in-game map). The regions are not the counties.

**Counties (in-game map):** Vice-Dale, Mariana, Kelly, Leonard, Lummox. Sources: Wikipedia [fetched], GTA6Info Leonida [fetched]. GTA6Info gives no region-to-county mapping. One fan site lists Ambrosia as a county; that conflicts with the others.

Home to gyms, bars and stores suited to roleplay (GamingBible) | GTA6Info Vice City [fetched]; rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida/vice-city [fetched]; GamingBible [fetched] |

**Other named places seen in launch coverage (region not always stated):** Hamlet (the M7 gang's town; Boone Speedway; police impound; Skeeter's bar; Deep Roots Memorial Sanctuary just north), Millport (Jacob's Tavern), Waning Sands (Leonard County; Frontier Adventure Golf), Belville (Leonard County), Grayson, Moreau (Lummox County; Lawton Co. County placement of Hamlet is inconsistent across sources: a fan wiki says Vice-Dale County, other guides say Mariana County. Treat as unresolved.

## 4. Vice City districts

Rockstar-confirmed names (trailers, official site): Downtown, Vice Beach, Ocean Beach (pastel art deco hotels), Little Cuba (panaderias; modelled on Little Havana), Stockyard (Stock 305, Electric Fang Tattoo), La Perle, Tequesta, VC Port, Vice City International Airport, Tisha-Wocka flea market (bootleg brands). Sources: GTA6Info Vice City [fetched]; search summaries of fan guides.

Names seen in post-launch find reports: Crosstown (Marbelle Hotel; police station), Decor District (NE 17th St warehouse), Washington Island (Trinity Apartments), Washington Beach, Vespucci Island (villa garages, Vespucci Mansion, Neptune Circle), Penmarch Isle, Harborside Park (Vice Beach), Southside (PTT Youngin$ territory), Peacock Bay, Catalan Blvd (Downtown), NW 4th Avenue (Little Cuba).

No source gives per-district wealth or danger ratings. GTA6Info explicitly says it does not.

## 5. Rich, dangerous and gang areas (explainer material)

There is **no in-game territory system** confirmed. Pre-launch guides said Rockstar had not confirmed gang wars or turf conquest, and no launch source describes one. Danger is better explained through factions, wildlife and police jurisdictions.

**Factions confirmed by GTABase** [fetched] https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/gangs/ :
| Faction | Type | Territory |
|---|---|---|
| Final Chapter MC | Outlaw motorcycle club | Ambrosia |
| PTT Youngin$ | Street gang | Southside, Vice City (Vice-Dale County) |
| San4San | Haitian street gang | Vice City |

Also referenced in find reports: **M7** (Hamlet junkyard, "not the safest location") and the **Kingfishers** (Grassrivers swamp, island fishing shack) [fetched: GamingBible, HotCars, OutputLag].

**Law enforcement agencies listed by GTABase** [fetched]: Vice City PD, Vice Beach PD, Vice-Dale PD, Port Gellhorn PD, Key Lento PD, Ambrosia County Sheriff, Leonard County Sheriff's Office, Mariana County Sheriff's Office, Leonida Highway Patrol, and POACH (Leonida Protection of Animals and Controlled Hunting).

**Rich areas** (by evidence, not by a rating): Vespucci Island villas and Vespucci Mansion, Pegassi Residences (supercar garage), Ocean Beach / Vice Beach waterfront developments, Mile Zero Air Station (military hardware). Wealth reading is [inference] from where rare cars and yachts spawn.

**Dangerous areas** (by evidence): Grassrivers (alligators, Kingfishers), Mount Kalaga (predators), Hamlet (M7), Mile Zero Air Station (military response), Hyde Island (hunter event), north Port Gellhorn at night (horror events).
