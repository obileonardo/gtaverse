# Wanted level and heat

Note: Numbers and states are as documented a few days after launch. Patches may change them.

GTA VI still uses six wanted stars, but the stars now carry states that tell you what the police know about you [1].

## The star states

| State | How it looks | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Cops Responding | Alert before any star | A crime was reported and police are on the way. Leave fast and they may never find you [1] |
| Suspect Unknown | Hollow stars | Police have a report but do not know who you are [1] |
| In Pursuit | White stars | Police have visual contact and know who you are [1] |
| Visual Lost | Flashing stars | Police lost sight of you and are searching. Leave the search radius unseen to clear it [1] |
| Heat | Red stars | You escaped, but police keep watching the area around your last known position, which is marked on the map. Being spotted or offending again there brings the wanted level back [1] |
| Heat and Suspect Unknown | Variant of Heat | Heat remains, but police have no description of you, your partner or your vehicle [1] |

## What the police know

Red heat icons under the stars show what police can identify: your appearance, vehicle, outfit, a visible weapon, your partner, CCTV and cloud CCTV [1].

Local CCTV footage can be destroyed. Cloud CCTV cannot, and eventually raises an alarm [1].

Bloodstains and exploded dye packs also make you recognisable [1].

## Losing the police

- Break line of sight and hide: ladders, unlocked doors, woods and bushes all work, and you can hold duck in a stationary car [1].
- Shoot tyres, and shoot at helicopters to make them retreat [1].
- K9 units can track you [1].
- Clear heat icons by changing outfit, hair or accessories, switching vehicles, holstering long guns and splitting from your partner. You can keep spare outfits in vehicles [1].
- Police are not shown on the minimap. Use Focus to spot them; the player arrow shows whether you are hidden [1].

## Wasted or busted

You lose pocket cash, a small BuckMe fee, unowned weapons and likely any stash bags [1].

Owned weapons can be recovered from your personal vehicle's trunk or an Ammu-Nation locker [1].

## How your Criminal Profile changes it

At the Psycho or Irredeemable profile tiers you can no longer surrender, and police are more likely to shoot on sight [1].

The Criminal Profile tracks how you commit crime, from Professional through Hardened, Violent and Psycho to Irredeemable, which is permanent. Each protagonist has a separate profile [2].

## What changed from GTA V

- A crime must be reported before police respond. In GTA V, police reacted anywhere [1].
- New: the unknown-suspect state, heat zones, disguises, attention from visible weapons and K9 units [1].
- Gone: police blips on the minimap [1].
