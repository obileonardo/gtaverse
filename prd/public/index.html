    01

    Summary

    GTA VI launched on 19 November 2026 (PS5 and Xbox Series X|S). Four days in, discoveries are scattered across YouTube, X, Reddit, TikTok and dozens of hastily built sites. Players open ten tabs to learn one thing.

    The outcome: one place where a fan can open the map, tap a spot, watch the original clip of what is there, see who found it and how sure we are, and follow the thread to where the same idea appeared in Vice City, San Andreas, GTA V or Red Dead. It stays current as fans find more.

    Why we win: other maps already compete on marker counts (GTABase lists 4,336 locations). None of them pairs each discovery with the finder's clip, credit and a verification label, and none connects GTA VI to the wider Rockstar universe. We compete on the story behind every pin, not the number of pins.

    Requirements

    • Opens fast on a phone held next to the TV: the first view is usable before any clip loads.
    • Clips load only when tapped (a thumbnail stands in until then), which keeps the map fast and avoids third-party tracking on load.
    • Every pin has a deep link, so a shared URL opens the map at that pin with its drawer open.
    • Filters by category, region, verification level and "has clip". Spoilers and mature content are hidden by default and remembered per visitor.
    • Pins are placed by us on the official map, from clips, and cross-checked against community maps such as Game Checklists. We never copy another site's pins wholesale.
    • The wheel zooms and drag pans, as on every map people know. Keyboard and screen-reader users can reach every pin through a list view.

    08

    Trust and credit

    The trust layer is the product. Four labels, used everywhere a claim appears.

    Multi-source

    Two or more independent sources show it, ideally with a clip. The default for anything we pin.

    Single source

    One credible report. Shown, labelled, and queued for a second source.

    Rumour

    Unconfirmed. Shown only in the myths tracker, never as a pin.

    Debunked

    Proven fake. Kept, because "is this real?" is a question people search for.

    Credit rules

    • The first finder gets the credit line when known; press coverage is listed as corroboration, not as the finder.
    • Clips are embedded from the original post so views and engagement go to the creator.
    • Never source from AI-written content farms, never link leaked builds or piracy, and never repost a clip.
    • A creator can ask to be removed or re-credited at takedown@thevicefiles.com or through the repo's takedown form, answered within 48 hours.

    Design direction

    What the research measured from Rockstar's own GTA VI site. This is direction, not the design: the look is decided from your picks on a taste board before any screen is built.

    Colour

    A night base of navy-violet (around #0D0C1A to #1F1F38) with aubergine frames, one loud pink accent (#F976B0), and the sunset ramp from blue-violet through magenta and coral to amber for hero moments. Six pastel gradients could each colour one region.

    Type

    Rockstar uses its own GTAArtDeco (condensed, heavy art deco caps) and Helvetica Now for body. We pick close, licensable equivalents. This page uses Big Shoulders Display as a stand-in only.

    Shape and material

    Pills and circles everywhere, frosted glass, soft fades of imagery into the dark page. Hand-painted loading-screen art in pastel skies.

    Motion

    Slow, scroll-owned cinematic reveals for stories; quick, quiet transitions for map controls. No bounce. GTA VI's site uses scroll-scrubbed video with smooth scrolling.

    Icons

    Near-identical original lookalikes of the HUD and blip style: pink route, star states, category blips. Drawn by us, never copied files.

    Moments of play

    Wanted-star states as verification badges, a WASTED-style screen for debunked claims, a phone UI for the social feed of new finds. Each one is optional and comes from the taste board.

    Architecture

    Recommended. Static first, so it is fast, cheap and hard to break.

    LayerChoiceWhy
    SiteAstro with React islands, built to static pagesEvery find and explainer becomes its own fast page that search engines can read. Interactive parts load only where needed.
    MapLeaflet or MapLibre on the official map, cut into zoom tilesSmooth deep zoom on phones without loading one huge image.
    ContentJSON and Markdown in the repo, schema-validatedPull requests are the review queue. No database until submissions outgrow it.
    SearchPagefind, built at deployFull-text search with no server.
    HostingCloudflare static assets on thevicefiles.com (rockstarworld.elvisobi.com also works)DNS for both domains is on Cloudflare. Static requests are free.
    UpdatesMerge a pull request, the site rebuilds and is live in minutes"Real time" enough for discoveries, with a review step that keeps fakes out.
    AnalyticsCloudflare Web Analytics, cookielessFree, no consent banner needed.

    Expected cost: zero on Cloudflare's free tiers. The only spend is the thevicefiles.com registration on Namecheap.
