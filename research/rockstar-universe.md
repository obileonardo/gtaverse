# The Rockstar universe: structure, connections, mysteries

Research date: 23 November 2026 (GTA VI launched 19 November 2026). Researcher: Research Agent B.
Structured version of every item: `research/rockstar-connections.json` (same ids).

**How to read confidence**
- **canon**: the in-game element exists and is documented by the game wikis or by Rockstar. For a theory, canon would mean Rockstar confirmed it (none are).
- **strong**: reported by several independent outlets or guides since launch, not checked first-hand and not confirmed by Rockstar.
- **speculative**: fan reading, a single source, or an unverified report.
- **debunked**: shown to be false, a hoax, or satire.

GTA VI items are four days old. Most come from launch-week guides (Leonida Verse, GTA Culture, KeenGamer, GAMINGbible) that themselves rely on Reddit finds. Re-verify each in-game, with a clip, before it goes on the live map.

## 1. How the universe is structured

**Verified (Rockstar, via the GTA Wiki quoting Rockstar's 2011 GTA III Q&A):** "...the 'universes' are the worlds interpreted at different definitions, 2d, 3d and high definition..." Source: https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Universes and press coverage of the Q&A at https://warpzoned.com/2011/12/rockstar-answers-your-questions-is-gta3s-claude-a-mute and https://ggrecon.com/articles/grand-theft-auto-multiverse. Reported summaries of the same Q&A say brands, radio and background characters can recur across universes, while main characters do not, with celebrities like Lazlow as the exception.

| Universe | Games | Shared continuity |
|---|---|---|
| 2D | GTA (1997), GTA London 1969 and 1961, GTA 2 | Liberty City, Vice City and San Andreas as top-down cities |
| 3D | GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, Advance, Liberty City Stories, Vice City Stories | One continuity, 1984 to 2001. **Manhunt** shares it (Carcer City; Manhunt 2 has a former San Fierro doctor as a lead) |
| HD | GTA IV and its episodes, Chinatown Wars, GTA V, GTA Online, **GTA VI** | One continuity, 2008 onward. GTA VI references GTA IV and V events (podcasts, memoir, trading cards) |
| Outside GTA | Red Dead Revolver, Red Dead Redemption 1 and 2, Bully, L.A. Noire, Max Payne 3, Midnight Club, The Warriors | Linked to GTA only through Easter eggs, shared brands and reused footage. Rockstar has never said Red Dead and GTA share a universe |

Other Rockstar games and GTA, in one line each:
- **Red Dead**: the richest web of references (Marston shirt and book, Red Dead street names, De Santa, the Sasquatch hunter, Capolavoro's Red Dead footage, RDR2's Mount Shann UFO nod to Chiliad, GTA VI's Waxman banknote and Arthur Morgan hat). Fun to map, not canon.
- **Bully**: Bullworth crest and "Canis Canem Edit" painting in GTA Online; "Hopkins" jersey in GTA V; Bully's car models reuse Vice City's.
- **L.A. Noire**: GTA V TV ad reuses L.A. Noire footage; Franklin's tattoo matches an L.A. Noire clue.
- **Max Payne**: "Max Paynt" garage in GTA 2; GTA V's Dignity yacht and Max's shirt; Max Payne 3 uses GTA IV screenshots for New York.
- **Manhunt**: genuinely the same 3D continuity (Carcer City), plus Manhunt mugshots and toys in San Andreas.
- **The Warriors**: GTA Online's "Come Out to Play" mode and a Definitive Edition achievement.
- **Midnight Club**: a Midnight Club: Los Angeles logo sits in GTA Online's "Conveyor" livery among other Rockstar eggs (GTA Wiki, Bully page). No deeper link found.

## Characters who cross games (20)

**Lazlow Jones, the radio voice of every era** (`lazlow`)  
Games: GTA III, GTA Vice City, GTA San Andreas, GTA Liberty City Stories, GTA Vice City Stories, GTA IV, GTA V, GTA Online. Confidence: **canon**.  
Real-life writer and radio host Lazlow Jones voiced a fictional version of himself on GTA radio from GTA III to GTA V and appears in person in GTA V and GTA Online After Hours. The universe rule's best-known exception: a radio personality who carries across universes.  
Sources: [Lazlow Jones (GTA Wiki)](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Lazlow_Jones)

## Places (7)

**Vice City exists three times** (`vice-city-three-times`)  
Games: GTA, GTA Vice City, GTA Vice City Stories, GTA VI. Confidence: **canon**.  
Vice City appears as a 2D city in GTA 1, the 1980s 3D city of Vice City and Vice City Stories, and the modern HD city in the state of Leonida in GTA VI. Same name and Miami inspiration, different continuities.  
Sources: [Vice City (GTA Wiki)](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Vice_City); [Vice City (HD Universe) (GTA Wiki)](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Vice_City_%28HD_Universe%29)

**Liberty City across three universes** (`liberty-city-three-times`)  
Games: GTA, GTA III, GTA Liberty City Stories, GTA IV, GTA Chinatown Wars, GTA V, GTA VI. Confidence: **canon**.  
Rockstar's New York stand-in exists in all three universes. In the HD universe it is the GTA IV city, referenced constantly in GTA V (Broker clothing, Liberty City licence plates, marathon chatter) and again in GTA VI (Liberty City Swingers card, a Liberty City memoir).  
Sources: [Universes (GTA Wiki)](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Universes); [Liberty City (HD Universe) (GTA Wiki)](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Liberty_City_%28HD_Universe%29); [Secrets and Easter Eggs in GTA V (GTA Wiki)](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Secrets_and_Easter_Eggs_in_GTA_V)

Mentioned on GTA III radio, the setting of Manhunt, referenced in San Andreas (Carcer Gas, Manhunt mugshots and toys), and in GTA V Michael says his first score was in Carcer City in 1988. Manhunt shares the 3D universe's continuity.
