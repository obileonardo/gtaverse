# GTA 6 mechanics: explainer material

Research date: 2026-11-23. Labels: **[fetched]** page read during research; **[cited]** claim from a fetched page that cites this outlet; **[snippet]** seen only in a search-result summary, not fetched; **[pre-launch]** official material from before release.

Most detailed post-launch documentation today lives in fan wikis (GTA6Info, GTABase) that aggregate guides. Rockstar has published no manual-style explanation. Treat numbers as provisional; patches may change them.

## 1. Protagonists

**Jason Duval** (voiced by Dylan Rourke, confirmed in credits per GameSpot and ScreenRant, 17 to 19 Nov 2026 [cited]).
- Official bio [pre-launch]: grew up around grifters, joined the US Army to escape a troubled adolescence, drifted to the Leonida Keys to work for local drug runners. "Jason wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder." (Red Bull partner page https://www.redbull.com/us-en/gta-6-jason-duval [cited])
- Starts in Key Lento, living rent-free in a house owned by his old boss Brian Heder. Personal vehicle: Vapid Ganado pickup. First playable mission: "Gone Fishing". Best friend: Cal Hampton.
- Source: https://gta6info.com/wiki/Jason_Duval [fetched]

**Lucia Caminos** (voiced and likeness by Manni L. Perez; GTA6Info calls this confirmed via a Game Awards 2026 nomination and Push Square reports her in the credits [cited]).
- Official bio [pre-launch]: her father taught her to fight; family roots in Liberty City; "ready to change the odds in her favor"; a life with Jason "could be her way out". Story opens with her in Leonida Penitentiary.
- Source: https://gta6info.com/wiki/Lucia_Caminos [fetched]

**Switching and loadouts:** you can switch between the two during free play (how freely outside story beats is not confirmed). Each carries two handguns and two long guns; spares go in your car trunk or an Ammu-Nation weapon locker (Wikipedia, GTA6Info [fetched]).

**Opening variation:** the intro differs depending on how Lucia is caught by police (Kotaku, 19 Nov [fetched]).

## 2. Wanted level and heat (biggest change from GTA V)

Source: GTABase guide, updated 21 Nov 2026 [fetched] https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-wanted-level-explained-police-system-guide

**Six stars**, but the stars now carry states:
| State | How it looks | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Cops Responding | (pre-star alert) | A crime was reported; police are on the way. Leave fast and they may never find you |
| Suspect Unknown | Hollow stars | Police have a report but do not know who you are |
| In Pursuit | White stars | Visual contact; they know who you are |
| Visual Lost | Flashing stars | They lost sight of you and are searching. Leave the search radius unseen to clear |
| Heat | Red stars | You escaped, but police keep watching the area around your last known position (marked on the map). Being spotted or offending again there restores the wanted level |
| Heat and Suspect Unknown | (variant) | Heat remains but police have no description of you, your partner or your vehicle |

**What police know** shows as red "heat icons" under the stars: appearance, vehicle, outfit, visible weapon, partner, CCTV, and cloud CCTV. Local CCTV footage can be destroyed; cloud CCTV cannot and eventually raises an alarm. Bloodstains and exploded dye packs also make you recognisable.

**Losing them:** break line of sight, hide (ladders, unlocked doors, woods, bushes; hold duck in a stationary car), shoot tyres, shoot at helicopters to make them retreat. K9 units track you. Clear heat icons by changing outfit, hair or accessories, switching vehicles, holstering long guns and splitting from your partner. Spare outfits can be kept in vehicles.

**Police are not on the minimap.** Use Focus to spot them. The player arrow shows whether you are hidden.

**Wasted or Busted:** you lose pocket cash, a small BuckMe fee, unowned weapons and likely any stash bags. Owned weapons can be recovered from your personal vehicle's trunk or an Ammu-Nation locker.

**Versus GTA V:** a crime must be reported before police respond (GTA V cops react anywhere); new unknown-suspect state, heat zones, disguise mechanics, visible-weapon attention, K9 units; no cop blips on the minimap.

**Criminal Profile interaction:** at Psycho or Irredeemable you can no longer surrender and cops are more likely to shoot on sight (see section 3).

Not documented anywhere fetched: military escalation tiers, bounty system, county-by-county police differences.

## 3. Criminal Profile (reputation)

Source: https://gta6info.com/wiki/Criminal_Profile [fetched], citing PowerPyx, GTABase, Respec, Tech4Gamers and GameSpot.

Tracks **how** you commit crime, not whether you are good. Five tiers: **Professional** (clean, no harm, tapes destroyed, alarms off), **Hardened** (forceful), **Violent** (civilians hurt, cops killed, finishing downed enemies), **Psycho** (mass violence, harming animals, driving over gravestones), **Irredeemable** (permanent; the Psycho icon cracks first as a warning). Each protagonist has a separate profile.

Effects: how Focus refills (stealth and non-lethal for clean profiles, headshots for violent ones, nothing for Irredeemable), police aggression, and one input into which ending you get. Rob Nelson told The Game Grid the icon can shatter and then never improve for that playthrough [cited]. Check it in the Jason and Lucia menu (touchpad or View) or hold right on the D-pad.

## 4. Health, Stamina, Focus and the body

Source: https://gta6info.com/wiki/Health,_Stamina_and_Focus [fetched]

- **Focus** (click both sticks): slows time while aiming and drains a bar; out of combat it stays on indefinitely and highlights enemies (through walls), interactables, cash, safe codes, animal tracks, trunk valuables and alarmed doors. Four levels, raised by shooting challenges, golf, pool, fishing and (for Psycho profiles) headshots. Weak points show red (lethal) or yellow (non-lethal).
- **Permanent boosts from secrets:** alien rescue (Health, Stamina, Focus), brainwashing chair (Focus), Satan boss fight (Health), Backrooms (Stamina, unconfirmed). This makes secret hunting mechanically valuable, which suits GTAVerse.
- **Training:** lifting raises Health; running, cycling, kayaking, paddleboarding and gym cardio raise Stamina. Gains fade if you stop. A fitness app tracks a daily workout window; a protein bar or shake afterwards locks gains in.
- **Weight:** overeating then sleeping adds weight; deficit and workouts remove it. Fully overweight unlocks the "Personal Best" trophy. Kotaku and X user @SirJosephWindsr popularised "Fat Jason" [fetched Kotaku].
- **Eating, sleeping, showering:** short-term buffs; visible fatigue after staying awake long (Wikipedia [fetched]).

## 5. Money and economy

Source: https://gta6info.com/wiki/Money [fetched]

- **BuckMe** banking app unlocks with the Chapter 1 mission "Buck Me". Before that everything is cash. Checking starts with a $15,000 cap that rises as you hit savings goals; Family Savings cannot be withdrawn; banked funds are shared by Jason and Lucia.
- **Cash limits:** up to $20,000 per character; vehicles hold only a couple of duffel bags. Unbanked cash is lost when wasted or busted.
- **Spending:** shops take pocket cash up to $999; bigger purchases go through the app.
- **Earning:** missions and side jobs (Pilot Pete deliveries $12,000 to $20,000), selling stolen cars to Moses (Chapter 2), looting trunks, robbing stores, planned bank and jewellery robberies (Chapter 3), selling stash bags to fences (example offers $14,400 and $12,720), hunting for Bret Vogel, and Randy's supercar **wish list** using the **Key Cloner** (Chapter 3; many targets carry trackers) [HotCars fetched].
- **Smash 'n' Grabs** and **Stick-Ups** exist as mechanics (GTA6Info category list [fetched]); details not read.

## 6. Phone, social media and the "no internet" design

Source: https://gta6info.com/wiki/Phone_and_Apps [fetched]

- **Snapmatic** returns as an Instagram-style feed. You can browse and follow but **cannot post**. Feeds carry ongoing storylines (for example rap duo Real Dimez) and are reportedly addictive "doomscrolling" (Kotaku item via X user @sickducking [fetched Kotaku]).
- **No in-game web browser.** Rob Nelson told Kotaku the web "didn't feel necessary for us" [cited] https://kotaku.com/no-websites-in-gta-6-vice-city-rockstar-rob-nelson-interview-2000757890
- Other apps: BuckMe (bank), RydeMe (rideshare, used as fast travel, with conversations), WAiNK (scan vehicles and landmarks; scanned landmarks appear in colour on the map), police scanner, Scooter Bros, Vice City Real Estate, Key Cloner, Reminders, fitness tracker, messaging, car delivery.
- The phone is a 3D object the camera zooms to, not a flat overlay [snippet].

## 7. NPCs, random events and the living world

- **Encounters:** Rockstar North says NPC interactions go further than RDR2. A designer quoted by Destructoid describes cooldown tiers: drive past and an event gets a short cooldown, partial engagement a longer one, full engagement flags it to never spawn again [snippet]. Interaction prompts are small and contextual rather than a dialogue wheel [snippet, Ginx]. Lasting NPC memory of the player is **not confirmed**.
- **Things happen without you:** crashes, heists and even plane crashes play out in the world (Kotaku via X user @biofalkner [fetched Kotaku]).
- **Animals:** pet cats and dogs (petting a cat gives a small Focus boost per GameSpot [cited]); 62 bird types (Insider Gaming via Kotaku [fetched Kotaku]); alligators drag and eat players; Wildlife Journal and "studying animals" mechanic.
- **Catalogue of random events** (dumpster lawyer, lawsuit burning driver, escaped polar bear, Operation Furball, Cool Dog, Jimmy the Drunk Sailor, Collateral hitman, UFO, Skunk Ape) is in `gta6-finds.json`; source page https://gta6info.com/wiki/Random_Events_and_Encounters [fetched].

## 8. Relationship system (Jason and Lucia)

Source: https://gta6info.com/wiki/Relationship_System [fetched]

Four unnumbered levels: Running on Empty, Partners In Crime, Crazy In Love, Hot Together. Raised by free-roam time together, dates (zoo, kayaking, beach walks, gym), crimes together, replying to texts and playing as both. No breakups or cheating. Rob Nelson: being lovers gives no "particular gameplay benefit" [cited GameSpot]. Guides report Hot Together is needed for the good ending on "Swim for It" (single-source).

## 9. Activities

Confirmed list (Wikipedia [fetched]): skydiving, backyard wrestling, basketball, kayaking, scuba diving, plus gyms, golf, pool, fishing, hunting (legendary animals, POACH), dirt-track racing (Thrillbilly Mud Club), dating. Retro games playable on the safehouse JoySystem console. GTA6Info's category list also names challenge families (Combat, Criminal, Exploration, Fitness, Shopping, Sports, Survival, Vehicle) and "Unfogging the Map" (map starts fogged).

## 10. Endings and missables

Multiple endings, reached by replaying the finale after the credits (Wikipedia [fetched]). Inputs reported by guides: Criminal Profile and relationship level. Missable unique gear exists (for example Donnie's H&L .38 in Chapter 1 mission "Don Gone", OutputLag [fetched]).
