# The relationship system

Jason and Lucia's relationship moves through four unnumbered levels: Running on Empty, Partners In Crime, Crazy In Love and Hot Together [1].

## What raises it

- Spending free-roam time together [1].
- Dates: the zoo, kayaking, beach walks and the gym [1].
- Committing crimes together [1].
- Replying to texts [1].
- Playing as both characters [1].

## What it does not do

There are no breakups and no cheating [1].

Rockstar North's Rob Nelson said being lovers gives no "particular gameplay benefit", as reported by GameSpot and cited by GTA6Info [1].

## Endings

GTA VI has multiple endings, reached by replaying the finale after the credits [2].

Guides report that relationship level is one input into which ending you get, alongside the Criminal Profile. This rests on a single source so far [1].

Note: The specific mission and level requirements reported by guides are story spoilers, so they are left out of this page.
