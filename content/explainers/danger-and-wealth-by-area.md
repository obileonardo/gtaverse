# Danger and wealth across Leonida

Note: GTA VI has no official danger or wealth rating for its areas, and none of our sources publishes one. This page reads danger and wealth from evidence: who controls an area, what lives there, and what turns up there. Treat it as a guide, not a game stat.

## No turf wars, as far as anyone knows

Rockstar has not confirmed gang wars or territory conquest, and whether players can capture or defend territory is unknown [1].

## Places that can kill you

- Grassrivers: swamps and wetlands with alligators and dangerous wildlife, plus the Kingfishers [2] [4].
- Mount Kalaga National Park: forests and hills built around hunting and legendary animals, where "you may end up the prey" [2].
- Hamlet: the M7 gang's junkyard [[find:gauntlet-hellfire-hamlet]] [3].
- Hyde Island: you can wake up in a shipping container with an explosive collar while an armoured hunter and his dog stalk you [[find:hyde-island-hunt]] [6].
- North Port Gellhorn at night: the Circles of Hell house opens <mark data-draw>between midnight and 4 AM</mark> [[find:hellspawn-satan-boss]] [7], and a hidden missing-tourists murder mystery starts at the Starlet Motel [[find:port-gellhorn-missing-persons]] [8].

## Where the money is

Note: Wealth here is read from where rare cars and expensive homes turn up. It is an inference, not a rating.

- Vespucci Island: villa garages, one hiding a car modelled on Brian O'Conner's Eclipse from The Fast and the Furious [[find:paul-walker-eclipse]] [5].
- Pegassi Residences in Vice City: an underground garage holding one of the rarest supercars [[find:grotti-cheetah-95]] [3].

## The police are everywhere, but not everywhere the same

GTABase lists these agencies: Vice City Police Department, Vice Beach Police Department, Vice-Dale Police Department, Port Gellhorn Police Department, Key Lento Police Department, Ambrosia County Sheriff, Leonard County Sheriff's Office, Mariana County Sheriff's Office, Leonida Highway Patrol, and POACH (Leonida Protection of Animals and Controlled Hunting) [1].

Police only respond once a crime is reported, and after an escape they keep watching a heat zone around your last known position [9].

Your Criminal Profile changes how aggressive police are with you [10].
