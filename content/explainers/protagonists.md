# Jason and Lucia, the two protagonists

Note: This page covers only what Rockstar showed before launch and the first hours of play. Story details stay out.

## Jason Duval

Jason is voiced by Dylan Rourke, confirmed in the credits per GameSpot and ScreenRant as cited by GTA6Info [1].

His official bio: he grew up around grifters, joined the US Army to escape a troubled adolescence, and drifted to the Leonida Keys to work for local drug runners [2]. "Jason wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder." [2]

He starts in Key Lento, living rent-free in a house owned by his old boss Brian Heder [1]. His personal vehicle is a Vapid Ganado pickup, his first playable mission is "Gone Fishing", and his best friend is Cal Hampton [1].

## Lucia Caminos

Lucia is voiced by Manni L. Perez, who also provides her likeness. GTA6Info calls this confirmed through a Game Awards 2026 nomination and a Push Square report of the credits [3].

Her official bio: her father taught her to fight, her family roots are in Liberty City, and she is "ready to change the odds in her favor". A life with Jason "could be her way out" [3].

Her story opens with her in Leonida Penitentiary [3].

## Playing as both

You can switch between Jason and Lucia during free play. How freely you can switch outside story beats is not confirmed [4].

Each carries two handguns and two long guns. Spares go in your car trunk or an Ammu-Nation weapon locker [4].

Each protagonist has <mark data-draw>a separate Criminal Profile</mark>, so how you play one does not change the other's [5].

Banked money is shared between them [6].
