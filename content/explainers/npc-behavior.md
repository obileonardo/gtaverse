# NPCs, random encounters and the living world

Note: Rockstar has published no manual-style description of NPC systems. This page describes what players have documented, find by find.

## Random encounters

Leonida's roads and streets throw up scripted encounters that you can join, ignore or turn down. GTA6Info keeps a running catalogue [1].

- A shady lawyer who lives in a dumpster gives legal advice and takes payment in pills [[find:dumpster-lawyer]] [1].
- A truck driver sets his own legs on fire to build a lawsuit against his employer and pays you through BuckMe to witness it [[find:lawsuit-burning-driver]] [1].
- A polar bear escapes a truck bound for the zoo. If you met the hunter earlier, he will buy the dead bear, so the outcome changes with your earlier choices [[find:escaped-polar-bear]] [1] [2].
- Operation Furball: give a fox-suited fugitive a ride, lose the police and deliver him for a cash or weapon reward [[find:operation-furball]] [1].
- A hitman who sounds like the lead of the film Collateral gets into your back seat and forces you to drive [[find:collateral-hitman]] [1].
- Jimmy the Drunk Sailor tries to mug you with a speargun; play along and you earn it [[find:jimmy-the-drunk-sailor]] [1].
- A cat in a stolen car attacks the driver [[find:cat-in-stolen-car]] [3].
- Two men invite you to shoot objects off a third man's head [[find:william-tell-target-practice]] [3].

## Animals that talk back

Parrots behind Three Sheets Liquor in Key Lento heckle you during a fight with the guards, and once the area is clear one tells you where cash is hidden [[find:three-sheets-talking-parrots]] [4].

## The encounter nobody can explain

One player filmed an NPC that survives sniper fire, makes a throat-slitting gesture, appears behind Jason and kills him instantly [[find:immortal-sniper-npc]] [5].

It does not happen every time, and Rockstar has not said whether it is a scripted scare or a bug. Only the original clip exists so far [6].
