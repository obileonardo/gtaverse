# GTA VI fan resources: the landscape four days after launch

Research date: 23 November 2026. Researcher: Research Agent B.
Method: fetched each site's live page text on 23 Nov 2026 (through a reader proxy where sites block bots) and read what the pages say about features, sources, credits and money. **Design quality was not assessed visually**: I took no screenshots in this pass, so anything about look and feel below is from page structure and the sites' own claims, not from viewing them. Treat design notes as unverified until someone screenshots each site at 1440 and 390 px.

## 3. Patterns across the field

- **Everyone has a map; almost nobody has the story behind a pin.** Maps compete on marker counts (GTABase 4,336; GTADB 2,808 community markers; MapGenie per-species pins). Depth per pin is thin.
- **Finder credit is weak.** Original finders (often Reddit users or YouTubers) are credited in press articles, then lost on maps. GTABase rewards its own members; Leonidaguide credits other guides; no map seen pairs each discovery with the original clip and the finder's handle.
- **Verification labels are emerging** (Grand Leonida, The Leonida Map, GTA Culture). Users are wary of fakes during launch week.
- **Lore is GTA VI only.** Leonida Verse's "Lore" is 9 characters and 6 places. The cross-game web (Vice City 1986 to 2026, Bigfoot from 2004 to the Skunk Ape, Red Dead eggs) lives only in wiki lists and listicles.
- **Legal posture varies.** Good: clear disclaimers (The Leonida Map) and editorial credit lines (Grand Leonida). Risky: a "Leaks" layer (GTADB), AI-animated official art (The Leonida Map), blip-style icons (GTABase).
- **Monetization norm:** the big players are commercial (IGN's MapGenie, Game8, GTABase memberships). Small fan maps mostly show no ads yet.

## 4. The gap GTAverse can own

**"The story behind every pin, across every Rockstar game, with the finder's clip."**

1. **Clip-first discoveries with finder credit.** Each discovery is an embedded clip from the original platform, the finder's handle and link, and a "first reported" date. No competitor does this consistently; it is also the legally cleanest way to show gameplay (platform embeds, see legal-fan-site.md 3h).
2. **Verification status on every pin**: verified in-game by GTAverse, reported by multiple sources, single report, debunked. Grand Leonida and GTA Culture show users want this; nobody combines it with clips.
3. **The cross-game lore graph.** Every GTA VI pin that echoes an older game links to that game's moment (e.g. the radio-tower egg to Vice City's Easter Egg Building; the Skunk Ape to San Andreas' Bigfoot myth and GTA V's The Last One; the Kelly County UFO to Chiliad, Area 69 and RDR2's Mount Shann). 85 seed items are in `rockstar-connections.json`.
4. **Myth and mystery tracker with honest status** (solved, unsolved, debunked), seeded from the debunked list so the site is the place that says "this one is fake".
5. **No leaks, spoiler-gated.** A trust position competitors with leak layers cannot take, and a legal hygiene benefit.

What not to compete on: raw marker counts. GTABase, MapGenie and GTADB are ahead and will stay ahead on volume. GTAverse wins on curation, credit and connections.
