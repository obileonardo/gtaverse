# GTAverse

A fan-made field guide to Grand Theft Auto VI and the wider Rockstar universe. Live at rockstarworld.elvisobi.com (in progress).

An interactive map of Leonida where every secret, Easter egg, rare vehicle, weapon, memorial and real-world reference is pinned, with a playable clip and credit to the fan who found it. Lore pages connect GTA VI to GTA I to V, San Andreas, Vice City, Bully, Red Dead Redemption and L.A. Noire.

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
